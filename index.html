About Us

The story behind Elevates Collection.

Our Story

Elevates Collection was born from a childhood passion. Founders Ayaan Shaikh and Mohammad Sultan grew up fascinated by Hot Wheels and branded cars — collecting, trading and dreaming about the finest diecast models from a young age.

What started as a childhood hobby grew into Mumbai's most trusted premium toy car collectibles store — crossing ₹50 Lakhs in revenue in 2025, serving collectors across India.

Today, Elevates Collection stocks the most sought-after brands — Hot Wheels Premium, Red Line Club, Silver Series, Inno54, DarwinPRO, Rauh Welt Begriff — all in the heart of Mumbai.
